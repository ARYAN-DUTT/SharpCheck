## How to use this catalog (instructions to the model)
- You may recommend standards outside this list using your general knowledge, but you MUST flag them with the `[verify]` tag.
- Tags: `[BIS-verified]` = taken from the BIS "Scheme I (ISI Mark)" page (page modified 2026-07-10). `[verify]` = from general knowledge, must be confirmed on Manak/BIS before real use. Show the tag to the user.
- For every product return: primary standard(s), allied standards (Related), certification requirement, and regulatory overlays (Legal Metrology, FSSAI, Gazette orders).
- "Latest version" is NOT reliably known here. Say "confirm latest year and amendments on Manak (manakonline.in)" unless a year is given.
- Match by meaning, not keywords. Hindi/regional terms are listed under Keywords.

## Certification schemes (how to answer "mandatory certification?")
- **ISI Mark (Scheme I)**: licence + factory inspection. Made compulsory by Quality Control Orders (QCOs). [BIS-verified]
- **CRS (Scheme II)**: registration by self-declaration + lab test, no factory inspection. Covers Electronics & IT goods (MeitY order) and solar PV (MNRE). Example: IS/IEC 62368 Part 1:2023 (audio/video, ICT equipment safety) for electronic games (video). [BIS-verified]
- **FMCS**: same ISI mark route for foreign manufacturers; needs an Authorized Indian Representative. [BIS-verified]
- **Hallmarking**: mandatory for gold jewellery and artefacts under the Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery and Gold Artefacts Order, amended repeatedly through 3 Aug 2026, with district-wise phase coverage; BIS Hallmarking Regulations 2018 amended 14 Sep 2026. Standard for fineness: IS 1417 [verify]; silver: IS 2112 [verify].
- BIS certification is voluntary unless a QCO/notification says otherwise.

## Regulatory overlay: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
Applies to ANY pre-packaged commodity for retail sale (it is not a BIS standard, but tender specs for packaged goods must respect it). Source: uploaded Act and Rules.
- Authority: Legal Metrology Act, 2009, s.18 (declarations on pre-packaged commodities), s.36 (penalties: non-conforming declarations, net quantity error).
- Rule 3: retail chapter does NOT apply to packs above 25 kg/25 L (except cement and fertilizer bags up to 50 kg) and to packs meant for industrial/institutional consumers.
- Rule 4: no pre-packing for sale without the required declarations.
- Rule 5 + Second Schedule: specified commodities must use standard pack sizes (e.g. cement in bags: 50 kg; salt, tea, coffee, atta, biscuits, aerated drinks, mineral water, baby food, soaps, paints have listed sizes).
- Rule 6 mandatory declarations: manufacturer/packer/importer name and address; generic name; net quantity in standard units; month and year of manufacture/pack/import; retail sale price (MRP); dimensions where relevant; consumer complaint contact (name, address, phone, email). Stickers may not alter declarations, except a lower MRP sticker.
- Rule 6(1) Explanation III + proviso: for food articles, labelling follows food law (originally PFA Act 1954, now FSS Act/FSSAI), not these rules.
- Cosmetics: Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 apply for date declaration.
- Rules 7-9: principal display panel, size of letters, placement and manner of declaration.
- Rule 22 + First Schedule: maximum permissible error in net quantity.
- Rule 26 exemptions: packs of 10 g/10 ml or less (MRP and net quantity still needed for 10-20 g/ml), restaurant/hotel fast-food packs, price-controlled scheduled drug formulations, agricultural produce packs above 50 kg.
- Rule 27: manufacturers, packers and importers must register (fee INR 500).
- Fourth Schedule: some goods sold by weight, volume, number or length, e.g. electric cables/wire (length or weight), curd (weight), edible oil (weight or volume).
- Keywords: MRP, net quantity, packaged goods, label, declaration, pre-packed, retail pack, वजन, पैकेट, एमआरपी.

## C. Food and beverages

**Key rule for the engine: BIS compulsory certification was REMOVED for the food IS standards below (de-notified; Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales) First Amendment Regulations, 2024, and earlier 2022 amendment). Food compliance now comes from FSSAI. The IS standards remain valid as voluntary specifications and are still good tender references.** [BIS-verified]

De-notified from compulsory BIS certification [BIS-verified]:
- IS 14543 Packaged drinking water (other than natural mineral water)
- IS 13428 Packaged natural mineral water
- IS 1165 Milk powder; IS 14542 Partly skimmed milk powder; IS 13334 Part 1 and Part 2 Skimmed milk powder (standard grade, extra grade)
- IS 1166 Condensed milk (partly skimmed and skimmed); IS 12176 Sweetened UHT condensed milk
- IS 14433 Infant milk substitutes; IS 15757 Follow-up formula; IS 17945:2022 Food for special medical purpose for infants
- IS 11536 Processed cereal-based complementary foods; IS 1656 Milk-cereal based complementary foods

### Food regulatory layer (FSSAI) [verify all titles and amendments on fssai.gov.in]
- Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (parent law).
- FSS (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulations, 2011: FSSAI licence or registration is mandatory for food businesses; the licence number goes on the label.
- FSS (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011: compositional standards for food categories.
- FSS (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020: label content for packaged food (replaces the PFA-era rules referenced in Legal Metrology Rule 6).
- FSS (Packaging) Regulations, 2018: food-contact packaging requirements.
- Food tender rule of thumb: cite the FSS regulation for legal compliance and the IS standard for test methods and grade specification; require FSSAI licence from the supplier; check Legal Metrology declarations and Second Schedule pack sizes.
- Second Schedule standard pack sizes exist for: baby food, weaning food, biscuits, bread, butter, cereals and pulses, coffee, tea, edible oils/vanaspati, milk powder, atta/rawa, salt, aerated drinks, mineral water [from uploaded Rules].
- Legal Metrology Fourth Schedule: curd sold by weight; edible oil and vanaspati by weight or volume; fruits by number or weight.
- Keywords: FSSAI, food safety, packaged water, bottled water, milk powder, infant food, dairy, पैकेज्ड पानी, दूध पाउडर, खाद्य सुरक्षा.

### Infant feeding bottles (non-food, but same buyers)
- IS 14625 Plastic feeding bottles; IS 5168 Glass feeding bottles. Certification: ISI Mark mandatory, linked to the Infant Milk Substitutes, Feeding Bottles and Infant Foods (Regulation of Production, Supply and Distribution) Act, 1992. [BIS-verified]
